Sudoku Steps: Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Sudoku Steps is an Android app that reads a sudoku puzzle from an image and explains how to solve it, one step at a time.

In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device.

What the app does with your information

  • Images. When you paste, share or choose an image, the app reads it on your device to find the puzzle. The image is held in memory while you use it. The app does not save it or send it anywhere.
  • Clipboard. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste screenshot". When the app comes to the front it also checks whether the clipboard holds an image, without reading the image, so it can tell you one is ready.
  • Your puzzle. The digits of your last puzzle and the step you were on are saved in the app's private storage on your device, so you can carry on later. The app turns off Android's cloud backup for this data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request the Android internet permission, so it has no way to send data off your device.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking code.
  • It does not request any device permissions, such as camera, storage, location or contacts.

Google Play

The app is bought and downloaded through Google Play. Google handles the purchase under its own privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown under "App support" on the Sudoku Steps page on Google Play.
